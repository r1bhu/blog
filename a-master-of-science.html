A Master of Science

Thu 08 January 2026

The years 2024 and 2025 have gone by in a whiz. They saw me set foot in a new country, be an audience to lectures from stalwarts on a variety of fascinating subjects, and add to my worldview, perspectives I wouldn't have considered otherwise. The punishing grad school routine keeps you on your toes, but there was plenty of seeing, learning and experiencing. And for all of my efforts I was bestowed with the honor of being deemed a 'Master of Science'. A Master. Of Science. To be clear, it was not:

  • Diligent Disciple, or
  • Trifling Enthusiast, or
  • Passable Practitioner, or
  • Wannabe Engineer

... all of which would have seemed more palatable than 'Master'. In common parlance, to be called a master of anything is high praise. To be a master of any craft is to be capable and confident enough to project authority in your niche, not just among lay people but also practitioners in allied fields. And yet, ironically, within the ivory towers of higher education, I feel that the title has been eroded of its semantics to some extent. From an outsider's perspective, to think that a twenty-four something student has achieved mastery, is a little hard to digest.

Now I'm not prescribing revoking titles or doing away with centuries old nomenclature. Certainly not after having spent two years of my life chasing that degree.
