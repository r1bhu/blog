A Master of Science

Thu 08 January 2026

The years 2024 and 2025 have gone by in a whiz. I saw a lot, learnt a lot, experienced a lot. And for all of my efforts I was bestowed with the honor of being deemed a 'Master of Science'. A Master. Of Science. To be clear, it was not:

  • Diligent student, or
  • Trifling enthusiast, or
  • Passable practitioner

... all of which would have sounded a lot more palatable than 'Master'. And while the higher education industry might cause us to not put too much stock in it, I believe it is essential to resist the erosion of meaning from the title.
