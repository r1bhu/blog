Title: A Master of Science
Date: 2026-01-08 10:00
Category: General
Summary: This is a sample post written in Markdown.

The years 2024 and 2025 have gone by in a whiz. They saw me set foot in a new country, be an audience to stellar lectures from stalwarts on variety of fascinating subjects, and complement my worldview with perspectives I wouldn't have considered otherwise. The punishing grad school routine keeps you on your toes, but there was plenty of seeing, learning and experiencing. And for all of my efforts I was bestowed with the honor of being deemed a 'Master of Science'. A Master. Of Science. To be clear, it was not:

+ Diligent student, or
+ Trifling enthusiast, or
+ Passable practitioner

... all of which would have sounded a lot more palatable than 'Master'. And while the higher education industry might cause us to not put too much stock in it, it is essential to resist the erosion of meaning from the title.
